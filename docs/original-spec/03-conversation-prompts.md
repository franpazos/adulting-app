### Decisions captured from this message
- **Documentation:** archive original prompts, keep a progress log, document everything in `/docs`.
- **Logo:** placeholder SVG generated by the AI now; final exact-render SVG to be provided by Fran later (replace `src/components/Logo.tsx` and the files in `/public/icons/`).
- **Database:** SQLite official build (`@sqlite.org/sqlite-wasm`) with OPFS persistence.
- **Router:** `react-router-dom`.
- **Currency:** default EUR, but **multi-currency required for debts**. Some debts are in USD with relatives — payment registration must support recording the amount in the debt's currency plus the exchange rate at the time of payment, and compute the cash impact on the source account. Fields added to the data model: `exchange_rate`, `amount_in_account_currency`, `amount_in_debt_currency` on `transactions` and `debt_payments`.
- **Google Sheets:** functional OAuth, not just scaffold (Phase 9).
- **Package manager:** `pnpm` (matches the sibling `fran-finance` project).
